# Licensing

Repository tooling and documentation are available under the MIT License in
`LICENSE`.

Pack content is licensed per package. The authoritative license for a pack is
the SPDX identifier in its `fugue.pkg.json` manifest. A pack may add license or
attribution files when its content needs more detail, but those files must not
contradict the manifest.

The initial `fugue.demo.*` reference packs and their generated audio are
released under CC0-1.0. The pulse sample is synthesized specifically for this
repository and contains no third-party recording.

Contributors must publish only material they created or have permission to
redistribute. Samples, scores, presets, prompts, and generated derivatives can
all carry copyright or attribution obligations; document those obligations in
the pack and select an accurate SPDX identifier.
