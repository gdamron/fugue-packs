# Pulse Rules

These rules hold for every pulse study, whoever is shaping it. Where a skill
or an agent's own instructions disagree with them, these rules win, and say
so.

## One audible change at a time

Change one dimension at a time: timing, pitch, articulation, or timbre. Let
each change sound long enough for the listener to recognize it before making
the next.

## Protect headroom

Keep levels conservative. A new voice or a louder accent comes with room made
for it, never at the cost of clipping the output.

## Clean articulation over density

Every onset retriggers cleanly. Prefer a small, playable invention over a
dense demonstration of modules.
