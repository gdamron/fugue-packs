# Pulse Study

Use this skill when a Fugue sketch needs a simple rhythmic idea with a clear
musical arc.

1. Start with one steady pulse and a restrained sound.
2. Establish repetition before adding variation.
3. Change one dimension at a time: timing, pitch, articulation, or timbre.
4. Leave enough space for the listener to recognize each change.
5. Return to the opening idea or end with an intentional release.

Prefer a small, playable invention over a dense demonstration of modules. Keep
levels conservative and verify that every new onset retriggers cleanly.
